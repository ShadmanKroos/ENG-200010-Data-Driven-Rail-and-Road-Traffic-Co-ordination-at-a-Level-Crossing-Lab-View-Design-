# Data-Driven-Rail-and-Road-Traffic-Co-ordination-at-a-Level-Crossing-Lab-View-Design

Rail Level Crossing Traffic Management System is a LabVIEW-based design solution for managing congestion and safety at a busy level crossing located between two train stations. The system addresses a critical intersection that connects major transit hubs, academic institutions, healthcare facilities, and a retail area, where removing the crossing is not feasible due to nearby heritage buildings. The design integrates sensors, traffic LEDs, train-wait LEDs, and boom barriers to coordinate road and rail movement, improve safety, and reduce bottlenecks during peak hours.

The system monitors train presence at both stations and measures road traffic using roadside sensors that count vehicles on both sides. Using real-time sensor data, it classifies conditions as Normal Traffic (fewer than 10 cars) or Heavy Traffic (10 or more cars) and applies scenario-based control logic. Under normal traffic, the train proceeds on a green station LED signal, and the boom barrier is lowered and the road red LED is activated when the train is 500 m from the crossing, then the intersection reopens after the train passes. Under heavy traffic, the train is held at the station for a set waiting period (station LED red), then released (station LED green), after which the crossing sequence follows the same safety logic as the normal case.
